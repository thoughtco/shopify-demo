@{{ shopify:customer:addresses }} lists the customer's addresses from Shopify, and @{{ shopify:address_form }} posts to the addon's address endpoints, which create, update or delete an address on the logged in user's Shopify customer (never one named in the request). Shopify's checkout offers the same addresses.

Delete is a DELETE to /!/shopify/address/{id}. On this demo every change is refused before it reaches Shopify, as everyone shares the account.
