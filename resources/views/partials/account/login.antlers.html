Your account, from Shopify

Statamic handles the login. The user is linked to a Shopify customer, and the addon's tags read that customer from Shopify's Admin API:

  • Profile: your name and email, kept in step with Shopify.
  • Addresses: the customer's saved addresses, which checkout uses too.
  • Orders: everything bought with this account, with its status in Shopify.
{{ partial:partials/under_the_hood title="logging in" }}

A Statamic user logs in with Statamic's @{{ user:login_form }}. The user's shopify_id links them to a Shopify customer: the addon sets it when a customer webhook arrives, or when a user is saved with update_users_in_shopify on.

Once you're logged in, checkout is pre-filled with your email and default address too (SHOPIFY_CART_BUYER_IDENTITY).
