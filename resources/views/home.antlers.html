{{# How the pieces fit together #}}

Statamic + Shopify

Shopify runs the shop. Statamic runs the site.

Shopify

Products, variants, stock, prices for each market, and a secure checkout. Merchants keep working in the Shopify admin.

Statamic

Pages, stories and layout, plus your own fields on products. Everything is a flat file you can version, and editors get the Statamic control panel.

The addon

Imports the catalogue, then keeps it in step with webhooks: a price change, a sale or a refund in Shopify updates this site within seconds.
