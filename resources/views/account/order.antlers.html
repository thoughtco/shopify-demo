The order's ID comes from the URL, so @{{ shopify:customer:order }} checks with Shopify that the order belongs to the logged in customer before showing anything. Anyone else's order (try changing the number in the address bar) gets not_found. The amounts are in the currency the order was paid in, formatted by the addon's shopify_money modifier.

Shipments come from the order's fulfillments, with Shopify's tracking details. Buy again passes the order's line items that can still be bought to the cart store's addItems(), which adds them to your bag with the same quantities, subscriptions and line properties.
