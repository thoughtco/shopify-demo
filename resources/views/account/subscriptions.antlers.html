Shopify only lets the app that created a subscription read it, so the addon can't ask Shopify Subscriptions for them. Instead, @{{ shopify:customer:subscriptions }} finds the orders that started a subscription (tagged Subscription First Order) and lists the subscription items on them.

The status and next order date come from two Shopify Flow workflows that come with the addon: whenever a subscription is created or changes, they write it onto its first order as a metafield, which the tag reads. Without them, you'd still see the subscriptions, just not their status.
